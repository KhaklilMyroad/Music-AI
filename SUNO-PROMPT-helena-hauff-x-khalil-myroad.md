# Helena Hauff × Khalil Myroad — SUNO 5.5 Master Prompt

Synthesis of Helena Hauff's raw analog electro/acid/EBM sound with Khalil Myroad's
industrial hip-hop / dark cinematic war-torn aesthetic ("Clash of Horizons").

## Style DNA (synthesis mix)

- 45% Helena Hauff analog electro / acid (303 bassline, TR-808/707, one-take raw hardware)
- 25% EBM / industrial techno (relentless mechanical drive, distortion, cold stabs)
- 20% Khalil Myroad industrial hip-hop weight (war-drum toms, broken-beat swagger)
- 10% cinematic Middle-East war atmosphere (sirens, sandstorm noise, haunted pads)

Vocals: near-zero, Hauff-style — two spoken-word fragments only, cold, reverbed,
buried in the mix (lines sourced from Khalil Myroad's own "Clash of Horizons").

## Research notes

**Helena Hauff:** analog-only production (TB-303, TR-808, TR-707, Juno-60, Alpha Juno 2),
often recorded in one take; electro/acid/EBM/industrial techno; Detroit electro roots
(Drexciya, Underground Resistance) + 80s EBM/industrial; *Qualm* (2018) — 60 min,
zero vocals; *Discreet Desires* (2015) — vocals on a single track only. Typical range
~125–135 BPM (occasional slow burners ~90 BPM).

**Khalil Myroad:** industrial / hip hop / electronic ("Clash of Horizons"), dark
cinematic war-themed narrative lyrics, verse–chorus songwriting, Middle-East imagery.

**SUNO 5.5 rules applied:** genre-first token ordering (first tag ≈ 30% influence),
4–7 strong descriptors, structure tags only in the Lyrics box, [Outro] + [End] to
prevent abrupt cutoffs, Exclude field for negative guidance, Duration Control for
exact track length.
