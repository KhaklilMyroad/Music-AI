# "Böyle" — Suno v5.5 Advanced Prompt (v3)

Turkish deep/afro house club track. v3 restructures the arrangement after audio
analysis of the reference track (Adam Port & Stryv — "Move"): inspiration, not
copying. Key findings applied: groove locked from bar one (no whisper intro),
a short surprise dip early, a long hypnotic plateau built by layering, ONE long
stripped breakdown late in the track (no kick/sub), and the absolute peak
arriving immediately AFTER the breakdown, then a layer-peeling outro with a
clean end. Sub-bass carries the track; percussion stays restrained; dark
rounded top end.

Kept per user request: 124 BPM, A-flat minor, airy almost-bewitching female
vocal with lazy behind-the-beat half-sung half-rapped reggaeton-style delivery
(no artist names). Kept from earlier versions: plucky bass, saz/bağlama,
hüzün, wobbling stabs, bit-crush, handpan, "Böyle" triple–stretch–cut hook,
FULL STOP + "HOPPA!" signature (now placed at the end of the long breakdown).

Verified by adversarial workflow (Suno format / spec compliance / artistic
coherence) — all passed after one fix round. Fix round: removed "no EDM drop"
and "riser" wording that could prime Suno wrongly, resolved whisper-intro
conflict, added belting/diva vocals to excludes, swapped "Eller yukarı" in
Verse 1 for "Omuz gevşek" to fit the nonchalant delivery.

## Reference audio analysis (Adam Port & Stryv — "Move", 5:53)

- Tempo ≈ 117–118 BPM (kept at 124 per user request); key G# minor (= A-flat minor).
- Groove present from bar one (RMS 0.39 at 0s); brief dip at ~0:20.
- Gradual climb by layering low-mid melodic content, sustained plateau ~1:50–3:20.
- One long stripped breakdown ~3:30–4:25 (~55s): sub-bass and kick vanish,
  onset density drops, only floating melodic texture remains.
- Absolute peak (loudest + brightest moment) lands immediately after the
  breakdown at ~4:30, then layers decay gradually to a clean end.
- Production: fat constant sub-bass, restrained percussion (density comes from
  bass/texture, not busier drums), dark top end (spectral centroid ~2.0–2.7 kHz),
  breathing filter movement.
