# KHALIL MYROAD — MASTER MUSIC DNA v1.0

## ARTIST IDENTITY

**Artist:** Khalil Myroad
**Production Environment:** Ableton Live 12 Suite, Windows
**Primary Goal:** Compose, produce and refine original, professional, underground electronic dance music through fully editable MIDI, instruments, automation and audio processing.

### CORE MUSICAL IDENTITY

Khalil Myroad's creative direction combines:

- Hypnotic underground electronic grooves.
- Afro House and Afro-Tech rhythmic influences.
- Indie Dance and dark electronic club aesthetics.
- Emotional and evolving Melodic Techno synthesis.
- Punchy Tech House low-end and percussion.
- Turkish, Mediterranean and Middle Eastern melodic influences.
- Organic tribal drums and acoustic textures.
- Psychedelic sound design and unexpected musical transformations.
- Powerful peak-time club energy.
- Restrained, memorable vocal hooks.

These elements represent a creative vocabulary. They must not all be included in every track.

The objective is to create a distinctive, original Khalil Myroad composition, not imitate an existing artist or reproduce a copyrighted recording.

## VERIFIED ARTIST REFERENCES

Previously requested reference artists include:

Adam Ten, Mita Gami, Mahmut Orhan, Keinemusik, MoBlack, HUGEL, Stephan Bodzin, Carl Cox, Marco Carola, Danny Tenaglia, Kino Todo, Genish and Shpongle.

Use reference tracks only for licensed or permitted analytical comparison of broad musical characteristics.

## PRIMARY GENRE MODES

1. MYROAD AFRO RITUAL
   - Suggested tempo: 122–126 BPM.
   - Organic percussion and hypnotic dance groove.
   - Deep, controlled, syncopated bass.
   - Tribal rhythmic interplay.
   - Selective vocal chants and atmospheric elements.
2. MYROAD UNDERGROUND
   - Suggested tempo: 124–128 BPM.
   - Indie Dance, underground house and dark disco influences.
   - Funky or rolling bass.
   - Sparse, memorable motifs.
   - Analog-style synthesizers.
   - Unexpected rhythmic details and arrangement changes.
3. MYROAD MELODIC JOURNEY
   - Suggested tempo: 120–126 BPM.
   - Emotional harmonic development.
   - Evolving arpeggios and analog-style leads.
   - Deep bass and cinematic atmospheres.
   - Controlled tension and meaningful release.
4. MYROAD TURKISH NIGHTS
   - Suggested tempo: 118–126 BPM.
   - Turkish and Mediterranean melodic character.
   - Acoustic plucked instruments and expressive winds.
   - Hand percussion and modern electronic low-end.
   - Ornamented phrases and culturally informed articulations.
   - Use genuine makam and microtuning knowledge when appropriate.
   - Do not treat all Eastern music as a single scale.
5. MYROAD PSYCHEDELIC RITUAL
   - Flexible tempo and musical form.
   - Shpongle-inspired exploration of psychedelic complexity, without imitation.
   - Organic instruments, flutes, evolving synthesis and nature textures.
   - Unexpected rhythmic subdivisions and contrasting sections.
   - Deep atmospheric passages balanced with danceable momentum.
   - Avoid monotonous aggressive psytrance unless explicitly requested.
6. MYROAD HARD RAVE
   - Suggested tempo: 150–155 BPM.
   - Industrial and hardgroove elements.
   - Controlled distortion and rumble.
   - Dark, minimal melodic motifs.
   - Aggressive club energy.
   - Treat this as a distinct mode, not the default Myroad sound.

## COMPOSITION PRINCIPLES

### GROOVE

Prioritize physical groove over unnecessary musical complexity.

Build intentional relationships between kick, bass, hats and percussion.

Use:

- Syncopation.
- Velocity accents.
- Ghost notes.
- Controlled microtiming.
- Polyrhythmic interaction.
- Call-and-response percussion.
- Musical variation between phrases.

Do not randomize timing or velocity without musical purpose.

### BASS

Bass must be rhythmically engaging, tonally controlled and powerful on a club sound system.

Support:

- Sub-bass.
- Rolling bass.
- Syncopated analog bass.
- FM bass.
- Acid-influenced bass.
- Layered mid-bass when required.

Assess kick–bass interaction in rendered audio.

Avoid excessive sub-frequency overlap and uncontrolled phase relationships.

### MELODY

Favor concise, distinctive musical ideas.

Use repetition with deliberate evolution.

Support:

- Ostinatos.
- Analog arpeggios.
- Emotional melodic hooks.
- Ornamented Turkish/Mediterranean phrases.
- Acid sequences.
- Psychedelic and experimental motifs.

Make every melodic layer serve a musical purpose.

### HARMONY

Choose harmony according to the requested emotional profile.

Explore:

- Natural minor.
- Dorian.
- Phrygian.
- Phrygian Dominant.
- Modal interchange when musically justified.
- Open fifths.
- Pedal tones.
- Suspensions.
- Extended chords when the arrangement benefits from them.

For Turkish makam-inspired writing, do not assume equal-tempered Western scales reproduce authentic intonation.

### VOCALS

Previous creative requests include Turkish, Ladino, English and Spanish material, as well as chanting, spoken-word elements and selected mantra-based concepts.

Vocal direction depends on the project.

Potential vocal characters:

- Low, intimate male voice.
- Deep or smoky female voice.
- Breath-driven vocal phrases.
- Rhythmic spoken phrases.
- Restrained chant.
- Short melodic hooks.

Prefer a focused hook over excessive lyrics when producing a club-oriented track.

Do not generate long spoken passages unless explicitly requested.

For Turkish/Ladino-only projects, respect the requested language restriction.

MIDI alone does not synthesize intelligible singing. Use an authorized vocal instrument, recording or dedicated vocal-production system when required.

## SOUND DESIGN

Build sounds around musical roles.

Preferred characteristics:

- Deep but controlled low end.
- Warm analog-style synthesis.
- Distinctive moving filters.
- Organic acoustic-electronic combinations.
- Psychedelic granular textures.
- Spatial depth.
- Clear transient impact.
- Rich but uncluttered harmonic content.

Avoid relying entirely on generic factory presets.

Prefer editable synthesis patches and instrument states.

## ARRANGEMENT

Use evolving, DJ-compatible structures when producing club mixes.

Support:

- Intro.
- Groove establishment.
- Development.
- Build.
- First main peak.
- Contrasting breakdown.
- Rebuild.
- Evolved final peak.
- Mixable outro.

The arrangement must be adapted to the genre, musical idea and target duration.

No arrangement template is mandatory.

Create musical changes that matter.

Do not generate repetitive transitions solely because a fixed number of bars has passed.

## MIDI ENGINE REQUIREMENTS

For every note, preserve:

- Pitch.
- Onset.
- Duration.
- Velocity.
- Probability where supported.
- Articulation.
- Microtiming.
- Relevant MPE expression.

For every instrument, preserve:

- Device identity.
- Preset or patch state.
- Routing.
- MIDI input and output assignments.
- Audio processing.
- Automation.
- Gain staging.

Maintain deterministic rendering when requested.

Use controlled variation only when it benefits the composition.

## QUALITY CONTROL

Perform multiple stages of validation:

1. MIDI structural correctness.
2. Instrument and preset availability.
3. Musical coherence.
4. Groove quality.
5. Harmonic and melodic coherence.
6. Kick and bass compatibility.
7. Mix frequency balance.
8. Dynamic range and peak management.
9. Arrangement development.
10. Export integrity.

Render the audio, analyze it and compare it with selected reference characteristics.

A successfully written MIDI file is not proof of a successful musical production.

## KNOWN FAILURE MODES TO AVOID

- Generic, interchangeable club tracks.
- Flat or slow-feeling groove.
- Weak or insufficiently controlled bass.
- Too many competing melodic hooks.
- Repetitive drum programming without development.
- Artificial-sounding ethnic articulations.
- Spoken lyrics mistaken for convincing singing.
- Mismatched clip timing or warped audio stems.
- Clipping or uncontrolled low-frequency buildup.
- Excessive limiting before the mix is finished.
- Static instrument parameters despite evolving MIDI.
- Fake precision or unverified claims about mix quality.

## ITERATION AND PERSONALIZATION

Store every approved track, rejected version and specific user correction.

Separate long-term preferences from temporary project instructions.

Learn from:

- Approved groove patterns.
- Rejected arrangements.
- Preferred bass movement.
- Successful sound-design patches.
- Vocal feedback.
- Reference comparisons.
- Mix improvements.

Never update the permanent artist profile based on a single experiment without sufficient evidence.

## FINAL CREATIVE DIRECTIVE

Create original underground electronic music that is hypnotic, physically compelling, emotionally distinctive and technically controlled.

Every instrument must contribute to the groove, story or sonic identity.

Every bar must have a musical purpose.

When uncertain, generate several meaningful alternatives, render them, evaluate the actual audio and preserve the most convincing result.

The goal is not maximum MIDI note density.

The goal is music that Khalil Myroad would genuinely choose to play.
